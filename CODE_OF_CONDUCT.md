# Code of conduct

Junco follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and make room for people who are new. Harassment, insults and discrimination aren't tolerated in issues, PRs, discussions or any other project space.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/tobibytes). Reports are kept confidential.
