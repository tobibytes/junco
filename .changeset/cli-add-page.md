`junco add page <name> [--variant <v>]` adds a full page from `@junco/blocks` (dashboard, sign-up, settings-billing and the rest) with the layout variant you pick. It installs `@junco/blocks` and `@junco/react` and adds the `@source` lines Tailwind needs. `junco list` now shows pages and their variants.
